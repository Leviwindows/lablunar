You run a lunar outpost

Ages 7 to 12 keep a lunar base alive for 56 days: 28 of sunlight, 28 of darkness, with solar storms in between.

Three numbers to watch

Energy — the battery stores what the panels make during the day.
Oxygen — the greenhouse and the recycler give back the air the crew breathes.
Radiation — the “dose” is how much radiation the crew has absorbed.

There is no perfect base: every choice has a cost.

Works on phone and computer. Nothing to install.
